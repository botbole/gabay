# Changelog

User-facing changes in plain language, newest first. Written at the end of each accepted slice. Sections per entry: Added, Changed, Fixed.

## Unreleased

### Added
- Product workflow: role-based agents, specs, backlog and decision log (internal tooling, no user impact).

## Shipped before this workflow

Listed by feature, with the version that shipped it. Specs in `docs/product/specs/`.

### Added
- Sign-in and roles (v3.0): login page, admin, gabai and congregant roles, protected pages, automatic session refresh, logout, rate limits.
- Weekly bulletin (v2.2): the week's bulletin as WhatsApp text, email HTML and an A4 print page, section toggles, WhatsApp share buttons for yahrzeits and simchot.
- Prayer schedule and lessons (v2.1): times from rules such as "15 minutes before sunset", special days, weekly text to copy, today's times on the dashboard and in the chat.
- Hebrew assistant (v2): ask and act in Hebrew in a chat (v2); prayer times (v2.1); answers limited to the user's role (v3.0).
- Dashboard (v2): totals, upcoming yahrzeits and simchot.
- Hebrew calendar (v2): Hebrew and Gregorian month, Shabbat and holidays, events per day, date conversion.
- Congregants (v1): registry with search, archive and restore, bulk delete, CSV and Google Sheets import; detail panel with related records (v2).
- Payments (v1): record payments, history per congregant, list of who has not paid.
- Aliyot (v1): assign aliyot by parasha, history per congregant, donation recorded as a payment.
- Seating (v1): seats with annual fee, assign and unassign, filter by free, taken and section.
- Yahrzeits (v1): records with Hebrew and Gregorian dates, upcoming list.
- Simchot (v1): records by type, upcoming list.
